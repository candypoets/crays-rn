# Screen 16 — Me

## Product contract

Me is durable identity and history, never the live-night dashboard. The first viewport identifies the validated local account with portrait, display name, abbreviated npub, and custody truth; expanding reveals selectable full npub and no secret. A gear opens Settings. **Recovery & backup** links to the configured recovery/settings truth. **My access** contains Tickets, Passes, and Memberships. **My history** contains Order history. Current room and active order are deliberately absent; those live only in Tonight.

## States and data

Protected account loading, missing, incomplete, invalid, read failure/retry, local custody, and NIP-46 custody remain distinct. Durable archive loading, refresh, offline cached data, empty tickets/access/history, and storage error remain distinct. Ticket/access counts derive only from trusted usable NIP-97 entitlements and protected ticket storage. Order history uses relay-derived archive state. No wallet balance, active-room status, or optimistic entitlement is invented.

## Navigation and accessibility

Profile disclosure exposes expanded state and a combined descriptive label. Full npub is selectable and wraps. Every row is a single 48dp-plus button with title/detail/chevron. The gear is labelled **Open settings**. Navigation is immediate and does not wait on a relay. Large type may grow cards vertically without overlap.

## QA

Unit coverage includes every account state, archive loading/offline/error/empty, local and remote custody, access counts, absence of live room/order, and all row callbacks. The registered Me journey independently verifies the device identity and durable relay projections; active-order interaction is owned by Tonight coverage.
