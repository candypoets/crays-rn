## Night Playlist treatment

Me is the durable backstage drawer: current local identity, current room,
active order, Tickets, Memberships, Wallet, Messages, and Settings. It is not
a fifth room mode and does not compete with My Night’s immediate context.

Directly below the title, a compact white identity pass shows the validated
kind-0 name, pubkey-stable portrait, shortened `npub`, and device custody. The
pass expands in place to reveal the full selectable public identity and the
visibility contract. It does not claim provider login, human verification, or
global publication, and it never exposes the protected secret. This adapts the
profile-card placement from the older canonical Me study into Night Playlist;
the current bright palette and single-column durable board remain authority.

## Motion contract

- Tab entry uses a stable large title and one compact identity pass before the
  current-room reveal.
- Identity disclosure expands in place without nested actions; names and the
  full `npub` wrap under Dynamic Type.
- Rows share one list transition; avoid cascading card animations.
- Opening Orders, Tickets, Memberships, Wallet, or Settings pushes a native
  route and returns to the same Me scroll position.
- On focus, durable counts refresh in place; loading replaces only the affected
  row and never blanks the whole screen.

No balance, ticket, order, or membership count is derived from local optimism.
